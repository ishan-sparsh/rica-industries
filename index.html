01 100% Pure Hass Hand Sourcing

We never pool general-grade windfalls or industrial scraps. Every container of Rica starts with perfectly sun-ripened Hass avocados hand-inspected for rich oil concentration (minimum 23% dry matter baseline), ensuring exquisite culinary density.

Primary Ingredient: Raw Avocado Strict Spec Verified
02 Proprietary Lipid Preservation (No Citric Acid)

Traditional brands flood dips with heavy citric/ascorbic acid to stall oxidation, resulting in a sour chemical aftertaste. Rica utilizes a cold-gassed custom micro-atmosphere sealing method, neutralizing oxygen while preserving natural avocado creaminess.

Acidity Regulation: Cold Gassing Pure Taste Preserved
03 Zero-Heat Oxygen Evacuation Sealing

Thermal processing ruins delicate lipids and turns vitamins bitter. Rica is packaged inside our high-tech cleanrooms using ultra-premium non-thermal oxygen evacuation, giving you the safety of pasteurization without ruining color, smell, or nutrients.

Processing Standard: Cold Pressure Premium Nutritional Integrity
